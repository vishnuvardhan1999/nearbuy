Delete your ShopNip account

You can ask us to delete your ShopNip account and the personal data linked to it at any time, whether you use the website at www.shopnip.co.uk or the ShopNip app on Google Play (uk.co.shopnip.app).

This cannot be undone. Once the account is deleted, you will not be able to sign in or get the account back.

How to ask

  1. Email hello@shopnip.co.uk with the subject “Delete my ShopNip account”, or use the Contact page.
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in, so we can confirm it is your account.
  3. We confirm by email once it is done, within 30 days.

There is no instant delete button in the app. We handle each request after we hear from you.

What we delete

  • Your account and sign-in details (name, email address, password).
  • Saved details such as your phone number, delivery addresses and location.
  • Your basket, saved items, and any messages you sent to support.

What we keep

  • Records of orders you placed (and any proof-of-delivery photos), kept for up to 6 years where the law requires it for tax and accounting. These are no longer linked to an account you can sign in to.
  • Any unused ShopNip wallet credit is refunded to you when the account is deleted: to your original card where possible, otherwise by bank transfer.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
